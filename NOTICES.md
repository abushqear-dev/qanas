# Notices

Qanas adapts ideas from the works below. It rewrites them in its own words and formats; it does not copy text.

- **Outcome-Driven Innovation**, Anthony Ulwick / Strategyn: the opportunity algorithm (importance + max(importance − satisfaction, 0)). Qanas rates the inputs from evidence instead of surveys.
- **Itqan**, Mohammed Abu-Shqear (MIT): Discovery's research rules (sources with access dates, freshness windows, two independent domains), the PER/JOB/COMP ID shapes and the voice.
- **market-gap-scout** skill (Anthropic skills): evidence → gap → opportunity ordering, the research lanes (complaints, demand, teardown, change), shapes of a real gap, the "why hasn't this been built?" filter, the wedge and the kill list.
- **feasibility-study** skill (Anthropic skills): writing the comparable-selection rule before looking, reporting caught and shown counts, and computing scores in code before quoting them.
- **voice-of-customer-miner**, Dean Peters, Product-Manager-Skills: clustering by underlying need instead of the blamed feature, frequency labels that cap confidence, source skew and switching triggers.
- **Value Proposition Canvas** (Strategyzer) and **ERRC grid** (Blue Ocean Strategy, Kim and Mauborgne): idea-generation lenses.
